Profile

I'm a digital designer with more than four years of experience across interface, web, and editorial design. I translate complex systems — management platforms, social intervention methodologies, document repositories — into visual products that people actually use.

At Ivolución I designed the interface for several digital platforms, from the En Cadena rebrand to the flows for Lukin, Mi Gestor and the +Listos project with UNICEF. In parallel I collaborated on pieces for the editorial line for UNICEF, United Nations Colombia and Fundación Alpina: design, layout and illustration for complete guides and methodologies.

I'm currently completing a Technologist degree in Multimedia and Web Development at SENA, in my work-placement stage, and I work as a freelancer in digital design, UI and content.
